# AI Usage Disclosure

## Overview

AI tools were used as a supporting resource during the preparation of this assessment to help organize ideas, explore alternative approaches, 
and improve the clarity of technical explanations. 
The submitted work was reviewed and adapted to the specific requirements of each task.

## Examples of AI Assistance

- **Test Design and Risk Assessment:** AI was used to help identify potential edge cases, organize test scenarios, and prioritize risks based on business impact.
  The final scenarios and priorities were adapted to the assessment requirements.
- **API Testing:** AI assisted in structuring Postman assertions and considering payment-related risks such as duplicate requests,
  timeouts, idempotency, and reconciliation. The distinction between executed tests and proposed additional coverage was maintained.
- **Automation Design:** AI helped organize the proposed framework structure and evaluate approaches to stable locators, dynamic elements, synchronization,
  and reusable components. The final design was considered against the constraints described in the scenario.
- **Release Strategy:** AI helped structure the release-readiness assessment and consider conditions for a go/no-go decision,
  along with practical actions and quality metrics.

## Decisions and Adjustments

AI suggestions were treated as options rather than automatically accepted answers. Suggestions were adapted where necessary to fit the scenario, 
available evidence, and practical constraints.

For example, the API testing work distinguishes between checks that were executed and additional negative 
or data-driven tests that were proposed but not fully automated. 
The automation submission presents an architectural design rather than claiming that a complete framework was implemented. 
The release recommendation is based on the risks and evidence stated in the scenario.

## Responsibility

I remain responsible for the final submission and the technical decisions it contains. 
I can explain the rationale behind the chosen approaches, discuss alternatives, and identify areas that would require further validation in a real project.
